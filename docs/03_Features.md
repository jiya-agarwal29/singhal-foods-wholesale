# 4. Unique Features (USP)

## ⭐ Quick Reorder

Business customers can recreate previously placed orders with a single click, making recurring wholesale purchases faster and more convenient.

---

## ⭐ Business Notes

Customers can include delivery instructions or business-specific requests while placing an order. These notes are visible to administrators during order processing.

### Examples

- Deliver before 9:00 AM
- Send GST Invoice
- Separate fragile items
- Deliver to back entrance

---

## ⭐ Dashboard Notifications

Whenever a customer places a new order, the administrator receives an instant notification on the dashboard, enabling faster order processing.

Examples:

- New Order Received
- Low Stock Alert
- Payment Status Updated

---

## ⭐ Email Notifications

Whenever a new order is placed, the administrator automatically receives an email containing the order details, customer information, and total order amount.

---

## ⭐ Smart Restock Suggestions

When inventory falls below a predefined threshold, the system recommends a suitable quantity to restock, helping administrators maintain adequate stock levels.

---

## ⭐ Low Stock Alerts

Products nearing stock depletion are automatically highlighted on the administrator dashboard, allowing timely inventory replenishment.

---

## ⭐ OTP-Based Password Recovery

Users can securely reset their passwords without leaving the application.

Flow:

- Enter registered email
- Receive OTP on email
- Verify OTP
- Set a new password
- Login with the updated password
